    Methodology

    Every screening runs a five-domain weighted ensemble in your browser: sanctions proximity (exact, fuzzy ≥90%, and affiliate-containment matching with corporate-suffix normalization), financial health (SEC EDGAR shareholders'-equity-to-assets, cached validation values), geographic risk (FATF call-for-action and increased-monitoring lists, Feb 2026 snapshot), entity opacity (registry identity + incorporation age), and industry-jurisdiction conflict. Domains with no data are excluded and weights renormalized — the system never fabricates a score. A direct match to a designated entity forces CRITICAL regardless of the composite.

    Data integrity notes

    Company records are real US companies from public datasets (S&P 500 constituents; NASDAQ listed-securities snapshot). No real company is ever labeled fraudulent by this tool — HIGH/CRITICAL results reflect matches against public government designation lists or documented risk-pattern indicators requiring human review. Profiles tagged [SYNTHETIC DEMO] are fictitious and exist only to demonstrate shell-company pattern detection. The designated-entity set here is a curated demo subset of 68 real, publicly citable OFAC SDN and BIS Entity List records; the production ingester mirrors the full lists (~15,000+ records) daily.

    V.E.T. is a decision-support tool, not legal advice. Escalations should be reviewed by a qualified compliance professional. Framework: Kanoklertwongse (2026), working paper.
